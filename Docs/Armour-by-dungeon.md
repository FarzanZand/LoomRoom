# Body armour by dungeon (sketch, 2026-10-22, fourth pass)

A ladder: the deeper you go, the better the armour that drops. Three pieces per dungeon, one per stage, twelve in all. What makes a ladder fun is in the drops around it, so most of this page is about how the next step shows up.

## What makes the ladder feel good

1. **Every step is felt.** About +25% armour a step, a new name and a new look. Smaller steps feel like nothing.
2. **The next step arrives a little early.** Each floor mostly drops its own piece, with a small chance of the next one. That surprise ("already?") is the best moment a ladder has.
3. **Old pieces fade out.** Once a newer piece drops on a floor, the older one drops rarely, so the floor isn't full of junk you have outgrown.
4. **Rarity is a side road, not the road.** A Unique (+45%) is worth about one and a half steps, so a Unique of the last piece sits between the next two plain ones: a real choice ("keep my Unique Studded Jerkin or take the plain Ring Shirt?") for a floor or two, then the ladder wins.
5. **A new step is announced.** The first time a player picks up a piece they have never had, the pickup feed marks it as new and the DM can comment (the Better Gear remark already exists).

## Drop shares per floor (a dungeon of three floors)

| Floor | First piece | Second piece | Third piece | Next dungeon's first |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 85% | 15% | | |
| 2 | 20% | 65% | 15% | |
| 3 | | 25% | 70% | 5% |

The 5% on the last floor is a glimpse of the next dungeon's material. A dungeon with more floors spreads its three pieces over them the same way (first floor, a third in, two thirds in).

## Small pieces

Helm, gloves, boots and leggings stay one per dungeon in its material for now (Leather 4, Iron 8, Steel 12, Crystal 16). If the body ladder plays well they can get the same three steps later.

## Build order

1. Dungeon 1's three pieces and the per-floor drop shares on the level (replacing Gear by floor's tier shares for body armour).
2. The "new piece" mark in the pickup feed.
3. Looks: one outfit per material, heavier with each step.
