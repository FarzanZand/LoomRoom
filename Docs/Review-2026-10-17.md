# Whole-game review, 2026-10-17

Seven reviews: Zelda (played the journey), Tifa (balance, from data and code), Ciri (art), Celes (UI/UX), Karlach (tech, measured), Samus (co-op), Guen (merchants, design fit). Nothing was changed during the review. Each item: severity, what, where, suggested fix. Numbers are for picking; duplicates between reviewers are merged.

Severity: **bug** (wrong behaviour), **major** (hurts the game noticeably), **minor**, **polish**.

---
