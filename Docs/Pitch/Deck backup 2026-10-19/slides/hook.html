THE TABLE WAITS

02

The premise

Narrative premise

“Welcome back.” You don’t know the man across the table, or why your bedroom door opens onto nothing but this room. He knows your name. He knows how you roll. On the table lies a dungeon, and when you step into it, you’re alone with a sword, a place you’ve never seen and things in the dark that want you dead. 

Four dungeons left, the man says, and he won’t tell you what happens after the last one. Die, and you wake in your bed. Survive, and you still wake up in your bed, but your room is not quite the way you left it. 

The logline. Read it as written: the apartment, the stranger at the table, the four dungeons, and the reset that the final act explains.
