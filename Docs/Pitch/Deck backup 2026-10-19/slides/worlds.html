How it plays

Two worlds, one table

The apartment

The meta world of the game. You live in this room, and the levels you play are generated on the table. As you progress through the game, the room itself and the dungeon master will change with you, as inspired by the amazing game Inscryption. 

The table

You will take the role of the pawn you place on the table, and control it from a first person perspective, still in the room, but in miniature form. Look up through the skylight and the Dungeon Master is looking back.

(Video)

Two playable characters: the room player explores the apartment and never fights; the table player is the figure in the dungeon. The apartment is always there, visible from the dungeon through skylights.
