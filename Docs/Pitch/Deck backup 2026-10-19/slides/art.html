Art direction

A clean pixelated look

Every creature, weapon and prop is pixelated and colorful. 

Low-res and worn, then lit in real 3D: torches, moonlight through skylights.

The room itself will set its mood with lamps and natural skylight from the room. Its visuals are just as important as the dungeon visuals, as you will at moments see and interact with the room from the table point of view.
