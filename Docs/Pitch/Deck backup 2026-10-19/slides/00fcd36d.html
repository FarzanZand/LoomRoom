THE TABLE WAITS

04

How it plays

The dungeon master will narrate you

The dungeon master

Starts off as a normal person, but will become more and more disturbing as you progress through the game. The dungeon master will guide you through the game and always be with you.

The narrator

The dungeon master will continuously narrate what you do in the dungeon. If you do well, he will acknowledge it. If you do poorly, he will mock you. If you try to throw a fireball at him from the table, he will laugh it off. 

Two playable characters: the room player explores the apartment and never fights; the table player is the figure in the dungeon. The apartment is always there, visible from the dungeon through skylights.
